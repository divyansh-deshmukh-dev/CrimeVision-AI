Responsible AI in Forensic Investigation

CrimeVision AI functions as an investigative decision-support aid. All neural detections, bounding boxes, transcripts, and cross-modal correlations are presented with verified execution provenance and confidence indicators.

Potential correlations identified across video streams, acoustic recordings, or digital photos do not automatically constitute legal proof. Every AI-assisted finding requires deliberate verification by qualified forensic examiners before entry into official court evidentiary filings.
